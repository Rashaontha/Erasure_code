### Limitations and remaining evidence

The ten-way CIFAR classifier is a coarse proxy for visual content. FID based
on only 100 COCO reference images will be noisy. Reduced CPU pilot training
and sampling cannot substitute for full 1000-step, 512×512 runs. EEBdeep uses
ten declared templates rather than the paper's reported 80. Full-matrix ASR,
target accuracy, retention accuracy, FID, and CLIPScore are **NOT MEASURED**.
